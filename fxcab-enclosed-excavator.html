Questions

About the FXCab Enclosed Excavator

Does the cab have heat?

Yes — a 12 V heater is standard. No A/C, but the large glass area and roof vent keep airflow comfortable in summer.

What is ROPS/FOPS?

Roll-Over Protection Structure / Falling Object Protection Structure. The cab meets ISO standards required on most commercial and municipal jobsites.

What trailer do I need?

2,800 kg requires a trailer rated for at least 7,000 lb — typically a low-boy or pintle-hitch trailer behind a Class 5 or 6 truck.

Can I add a breaker or thumb?

65 L/min at 21 MPa handles heavy-duty breakers, industrial thumbs and forestry mulchers.
